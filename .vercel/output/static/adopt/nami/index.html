Nami

Age

3.5 years

Size

medium

Sex

female

Nami was born in March 2023 and found at just two and a half months old by a close family friend of Vesna, who runs our shelter. She grew up in a countryside environment alongside cats, chickens, goats, and other dogs, learning from a young age how to share space with different animals. Nami is incredibly affectionate, social, and full of love. She adores human attention, cuddles, and being close to people. She is comfortable in a car, familiar with walking on a lead, and is playful, intelligent, and very agile. One thing to know: Nami is a talented escape artist and can jump over fences, so she needs a home with secure, high fencing or an apartment with regular walks and exercise.

Transport costs

There is no adoption fee — the dog is free. You only cover the cost of transport to your home: licensed transport, pet passport processing, and deworming based on your country's requirements. Every dog is already neutered, vaccinated, and microchipped before travel. The cost is the same for any dog.

United Kingdom

~£500

Europe

€300–400

Switzerland

CHF 500–550

Dogs travelling to the UK will be tested for brucellosis as required for entry. Other health tests are available on request.

Fees may vary slightly depending on transport costs at the time of adoption. Every dog travels with a passport, fully vaccinated, neutered, dewormed, and flea-treated before departure. We recommend budgeting for a vet check-up once your dog arrives and settles in.
