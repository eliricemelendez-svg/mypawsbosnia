Freya

Age

3 years

Size

medium

Sex

female

Freya came to us from a public shelter in terrible condition. She was frightened, freezing, and overwhelmed. She arrived with her mother, brother, and two sisters. One by one, they all found homes. Everyone except Freya. She is gentle, sensitive, and loving. She is great with children, cats, and other dogs. She can lack confidence in new situations, so she would do best in a home with another calm, confident dog who can help her settle in. She already knows the basics of walking on a leash.

Transport costs

There is no adoption fee — the dog is free. You only cover the cost of transport to your home: licensed transport, pet passport processing, and deworming based on your country's requirements. Every dog is already neutered, vaccinated, and microchipped before travel. The cost is the same for any dog.

Switzerland

Dogs travelling to the UK will be tested for brucellosis as required for entry. Other health tests are available on request.

Fees may vary slightly depending on transport costs at the time of adoption. Every dog travels with a passport, fully vaccinated, neutered, dewormed, and flea-treated before departure. We recommend budgeting for a vet check-up once your dog arrives and settles in.
