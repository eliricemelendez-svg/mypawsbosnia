Your impact

Every donation makes a direct difference. Here is where your support goes.

Food

Feeding 60 dogs requires regular bulk deliveries of dry and wet food. Donations cover monthly food purchases and delivery to the shelter.

Veterinary care

Every dog receives vaccinations, deworming, microchipping, and spay/neuter surgery. Emergency treatments and surgeries are also covered by donations.

Shelter improvements

Fencing, kennels, weatherproofing, and general upkeep of the property where the dogs live.
